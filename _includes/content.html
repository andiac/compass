About Me

I'm a machine learning research associate at the Centre for AI Fundamentals, University of Manchester, working under Prof. Samuel Kaski and funded by UKRI Generative AI Hub. I completed my PhD at the University of Cambridge under Dr. Damon Wischik. My educational background includes a BSc in Computer Science and Mathematics from Manchester, followed by an MPhil in Computer Science and an MASt in Mathematics from Cambridge. For a complete overview of my academic and professional journey, please refer to my CV.

I'm passionate about learning and creative problem-solving. I'm always open to collaboration - feel free to reach out to me at az381 [at] cantab [dot] ac [dot] uk.
